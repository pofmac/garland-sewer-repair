Hydro Jetting & Main Line Clogs in Garland, TX

High-pressure water scours grease, sludge and roots out of the main sewer line.

What it is

Hydro jetting sends water at very high pressure through a special nozzle that cleans the full width of the pipe, not just a hole through the clog like a cable snake. It is the usual fix for grease buildup and for clearing roots before a repair or lining.

Jetting is not safe for every pipe. Very old, cracked or collapsed pipes can be damaged further, so a good plumber inspects with a camera first.

When it's needed

  • Slow drains throughout the house
  • Grease buildup in kitchen and main lines
  • Roots that keep coming back after snaking
  • Cleaning the line before a camera inspection or lining

Typical cost

Hydro jetting a main line typically costs about $350 to $1,000.

Questions

Is hydro jetting better than snaking?

For grease and roots, usually yes, because it cleans the whole pipe wall. Snaking is cheaper and fine for a simple blockage.
