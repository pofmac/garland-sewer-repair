What changes the price

Length and depth

Longer, deeper lines cost more to dig and replace.

What's on top

Driveways, patios, slabs and big trees add cost to digging, which is where trenchless can save money overall.

Pipe material and condition

A collapsed line limits the options; a line that can be lined may cost less.

Permits and the city tie-in

Permits, inspections and connecting at the city side add to the total.
