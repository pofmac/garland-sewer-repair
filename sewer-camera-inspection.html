Sewer Camera Inspection in Garland, TX

A small waterproof camera goes down the line so you can see the problem before paying to fix it.

📞 Phone line opening soon

What it is

The plumber feeds a camera on a flexible cable through a cleanout or drain. You see the inside of the pipe on a screen: roots, cracks, sags, grease or a collapse, and how far along the line each problem is. Many plumbers also locate the problem spot from above so any digging is in exactly the right place.

When it's needed

  • Before buying an older home (a 'sewer scope' is a common add-on to a home inspection)
  • After a backup, to find out why it happened
  • Before agreeing to an expensive replacement, for a second opinion
  • Clogs keep coming back

Typical cost

A camera inspection usually costs about $250 to $900, and is sometimes included if you go ahead with the repair.

Questions

Can I get a copy of the video?

Ask for it. Many plumbers will send the video or screenshots, which is useful for a second opinion or for a home sale.

Should I get a sewer scope before buying a house?

For homes built before about 1980, it is widely recommended. Repairing a failed line can cost thousands, and a standard home inspection does not look inside the sewer line.
