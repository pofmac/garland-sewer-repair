Sewage coming up through floor drains, tubs or toilets needs fast attention.

What it is

A backup means wastewater can't get out to the city sewer. The cause is usually a blocked or broken main line on your property, but sometimes it's the city main. A plumber can clear the line and find the cause so it doesn't happen again.

What to do right now

  1. Stop using water: no flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher
  2. Keep children and pets away from the water; sewage carries germs
  3. If water is near outlets or appliances, stay out and switch off power only if you can do so safely
  4. Call a plumber to clear the line
  5. If neighbors are backing up too, call the City of Garland, since the public main may be blocked
  6. Take photos for your insurance company

Typical cost

Clearing a main-line clog often costs a few hundred dollars. If a camera shows broken pipe, repair or replacement is priced separately.

Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover a sewer backup?

Many standard policies don't cover sewer backup unless you added a 'water backup' endorsement. Check your policy or call your agent.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

Small, clean-water spills are one thing. Sewage carries bacteria; for anything more than a little, consider a professional water damage and cleanup company.
