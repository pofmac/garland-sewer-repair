Sewer Line Replacement in Garland, TX

When a sewer line is collapsed, badly cracked or full of roots, replacing it ends the repeat clogs.

📞 Phone line opening soon

What it is

Replacement means taking out the old pipe from the house to the city connection and putting in new PVC pipe, which does not rust and resists roots far better than cast iron or clay. It can be done by digging a trench, or with trenchless methods if the yard, driveway or trees are in the way.

When it's needed

  • A camera inspection shows a collapsed, crushed or badly offset pipe
  • Cast iron that is scaling, rusted through or flattened
  • Orangeburg or clay pipe with many root intrusions
  • Repeated backups even after the line has been cleaned

Typical cost

Full replacement commonly runs from about $3,000 to $25,000. Length of the line, depth, what is above it (driveway, patio, mature trees) and the method all change the price.

Questions

How long does sewer line replacement take?

Most home sewer lines are replaced in one to three days. Trenchless jobs are often finished in a single day.

Do I need a permit in Garland?

Sewer line work generally needs a permit and an inspection, and in Texas the work must be done by a plumber licensed by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. Your plumber normally pulls the permit.

Who is responsible for the sewer line?

In most cities, including Garland, the homeowner is responsible for the private line from the house to the city's connection, and the city maintains the public main. Ask your plumber or the City of Garland if you are unsure where your part ends.
